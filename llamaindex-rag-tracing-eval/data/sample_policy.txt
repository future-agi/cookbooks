Acme Corp Remote Work Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2025

1. Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day onboarding period are
eligible to work remotely up to 3 days per week. Part-time employees may work
remotely with manager approval.

2. Equipment
The company will provide a laptop and necessary peripherals for remote work.
Employees are responsible for maintaining a reliable internet connection with
a minimum speed of 25 Mbps download and 10 Mbps upload.

3. Core Hours
All employees must be available and responsive between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM
in their local timezone, Monday through Friday. Meetings should be scheduled
within these core hours whenever possible.

4. Security
Employees must use the company VPN when accessing internal systems remotely.
Public Wi-Fi networks must not be used without VPN protection. All work must
be performed on company-issued devices.

5. Expenses
The company provides a $50/month stipend to offset home office costs such as
internet and electricity. This stipend is included in the monthly payroll.

6. Performance
Remote employees are evaluated on output and results, not hours logged.
Managers will conduct bi-weekly check-ins with remote team members.

7. Violations
Failure to comply with this policy may result in loss of remote work
privileges and disciplinary action up to and including termination.
